Adopts engine 0.30.0's progression seams in Deepward and Drift Foundry, so a career's money and standing live in one shared system and a locked shelf is locked by standing rather than by a per-game rule.

- Deepward: extraction scrip banks on the rail and is lost with a Life; Marrow standing rises on a return and falls on a death; the fitter's counter prices each good from a curve read at live standing and refuses locked shelves until standing opens them.
- Drift Foundry: yard credits bank between runs and a crushed haul is lost; each slot ladder prices its rungs from one curve; the yard refuses EV conversion, monster treads and armor plating until standing reaches them.
- Both: one authored table per game, every gate's reward id validated at load, money/standing/unlocks in the save, deterministic tests for the settle paths.
- Deepward's save moves to version 3 and migrates version 1 and 2 records; purchased supplies carry their own instance ids.

Engine seam: packages/core/src/progression (adopts #2038).

Closes #2038 gaps: D1.5, D1.7, D2.7, D2.10, D3.4, D3.8, D4.2, D5.1, D5.6, D6.4, D7.4, D7.5, D8.4 all rebuild currency, unlocks, prices and a career bank; this is the one system they share.
