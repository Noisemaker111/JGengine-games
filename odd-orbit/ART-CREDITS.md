# Odd Orbit art

The moon-slug bodies, shell bands, signal stalks, eyes, padded feet, habitat appliances, crystal clusters, plants, low walls and deck markings in `AlienMesh.tsx` and `HabitatArt.tsx` are original procedural geometry authored for Odd Orbit, under the repository license.

The existing published asset catalog and its Quaternius, KayKit and ambientCG provenance remain intact. This presentation renders native geometry instead of requesting those catalog models or terrain maps. See the repository's CONTENT-PROVENANCE.md for retained third-party attribution. No legal clearance claim is made.

First orbit is an optional settlement charter. A missed charter preserves the household and offers another attempt or open-ended living. Device saves use the new `odd-orbit.household.v1` key; they do not remove any previous storage keys. The prior version did not enable household persistence.
