The dispatch booth, parcel towers, ringed coconut palm and Mainsail poster are original Harbor Heat art authored for this repository. The GLBs use geometry and flat PBR materials; they contain no external textures or third-party meshes. `node scripts/author-harbor.mjs` rebuilds the five models from the readable source, with copyright metadata embedded. The repository license applies.

Existing KayKit, Quaternius and ambientCG assets retain their original credits and licenses. These additions do not replace those attributions.
