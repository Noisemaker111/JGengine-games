The dispatch booth, parcel towers, ringed coconut palm and Mainsail poster are original Harbor Heat art authored for this repository. The GLBs use geometry and flat PBR materials; they contain no external textures or third-party meshes. `node scripts/author-harbor.mjs` rebuilds the five models from the readable source, with copyright metadata embedded. The repository license applies.

`mainsail-palm-far.glb` is generated, not authored: it is the level Harbor Heat draws past 60 m. `node scripts/build-far-meshes.mjs` reads `mainsail-palm.glb` and writes it. Closed solids (trunk segments, coconuts) take quadric-edge collapse and open sheets (the fronds) keep every vertex, because the crown outline is the silhouette. Vertices on the bounding box are locked, so origin, footprint, pivot and the height `targetHeight` is measured from are unchanged; the far level measures 95% of the full mesh's silhouette across eight yaws and all three street heights.

`city-block.glb` is the unit building block (1 m cube, base at y = 0) every city building draws: `node scripts/author-city-block.mjs` rebuilds it. Its sides carry the material `facade` and its top `roof`; each placement stretches it to its lot with `visual.scale` and lays an ambientCG facade scan (brick red, grey and brown, blue and mirror glass, all CC0) over `facade` at real-world tile size. It has no textures of its own.

Street furniture comes from the engine's realistic model library (CC0 scans from Poly Haven), pulled by `bun run provision harbor-heat` into `public/models/library/`; `src/game/world/props.ts` maps each prop to its library name and builds the credits from the same names. Every model's source, author and license is listed in the repository's `CONTENT-PROVENANCE.md`.

Existing KayKit, Quaternius and ambientCG assets retain their original credits and licenses. These additions do not replace those attributions.
