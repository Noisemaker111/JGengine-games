The dispatch booth, parcel towers, ringed coconut palm and Mainsail poster are original Harbor Heat art authored for this repository. The GLBs use geometry and flat PBR materials; they contain no external textures or third-party meshes. `node scripts/author-harbor.mjs` rebuilds the five models from the readable source, with copyright metadata embedded. The repository license applies.

The two far-detail meshes are generated, not authored: `mainsail-palm-far.glb` and `planter-far.glb` are the level Harbor Heat draws past 60 m. `node scripts/build-far-meshes.mjs` reads `mainsail-palm.glb` and the provisioned `Bush_Common_Flowers.glb` and writes both, so run `bun run provision harbor-heat` first on a fresh checkout. The script decides per surface: closed solids (trunk segments, coconuts) take quadric-edge collapse, loose cards (the planter's 450 leaf cards and 36 flower heads) keep a spatially spread subset, and open sheets (the palm's fronds) keep every vertex because the crown outline is the silhouette. Vertices on each model's bounding box are locked, so origin, footprint, pivot and the height `targetHeight` is measured from are unchanged - the palm's far level measures 95% of the full mesh's silhouette across eight yaws and all three street heights.

Existing KayKit, Quaternius and ambientCG assets retain their original credits and licenses. These additions do not replace those attributions.
