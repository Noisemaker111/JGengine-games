# Wayfarer Deck

An original illustrated deckbuilder about crossing a weathered mountain road. Five foes have distinct silhouettes and intent patterns: Pitch Ooze, Cairn Whisperer, Ridge Borer, Iron Sentry and Gate Colossus. The landscape and creature portraits are original SVG geometry in `src/game/ui/RoadArt.tsx`; cards use the existing authored icon set. Existing repository and engine attribution remains applicable.

Run `bun run dev` in this directory. `bun run check-types` and `bun run test` verify the published engine consumer. Vite resolves published `@jgengine/*` packages even when the game is hosted beside engine source.

Click or tap cards, or press 1–9 in hand order. F ends the turn. Escape pauses/resumes. Settings offers larger card text and reduced motion, both saved on this device. The card strip scrolls on narrow screens. Each turn grants three energy and five cards. Read the intent before spending energy; Block resets on your next turn, Weak reduces outgoing damage by 25%, and Vulnerable increases incoming damage by 50%. Strength resets between battles.

New crossings have four battles with branching stops. After the Pitch Ooze, choose a finite-stock shop or help a stranded porter; then face either the Cairn Whisperer or Ridge Borer. Choose healing or a card upgrade at shelter, or accept a costly cairn oath. The Iron Sentry leads to a final trader or fire before the Gate Colossus. Rewards, wounds, spent coins, upgrades and event consequences travel with the deck. Shops sell cards for 20 coins, a 14-HP tonic for 12, and card removal for 14. Rest offers 18 HP or one explicit upgrade. Event health costs cannot kill the traveler.

After a win, choose a card, recover up to 12 HP instead, or continue without either. The first reward includes guard/counter, Strength/multi-hit and tempo foundations. Later rewards and shop stock are seeded. Guard converts protection into damage; Strength improves repeated hits; tempo cycles and cleanses the deck. Defeat offers a fresh crossing. New crossings require confirmation while another crossing is active.

Runs save after actions to the browser-local key `wayfarer-deck.road-save.v1` using the bounded v2 schema. Existing v1 checkpoints retain their original five-fight path without rerolling. The checkpoint includes ordered card zones, turn pools, health/statuses, reward choices, route, stock, spending, upgrades and journal. Reload opens the start screen with Continue; gameplay stays gated until the player resumes. Unreadable saves remain untouched until the player explicitly begins a new crossing. No engine save namespaces or backend records are changed. Browser storage denial is shown in the HUD. Saving is local to the current browser origin; cross-device/cloud saving is not provided.
