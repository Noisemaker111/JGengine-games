# Field Station art notes

The survey expedition's character and equipment are original code-authored geometry in `src/game/FieldEquipment.tsx`: the field surveyor and articulated limbs, tripod readout instruments, anemometer, tally pennants, shoreline pier, thermal vent stones and stakes, and solar-roof field shelter. Materials use a prairie palette of oxidized copper, canvas ivory, deep green and instrument cyan. No external models, textures or fonts were added.

The existing environment scene, studio integrations and published JG Engine dependencies retain their upstream licenses and attribution. See the repository's content provenance and the installed packages' license files. These additions do not replace or alter those credits.
